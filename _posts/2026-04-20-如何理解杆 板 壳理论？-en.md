## The general theory of rod (1968 Green&Laws)

We consider a curve in three-dimensional space, defined as:

$$
\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{r}(\theta,t)
$$

$\theta$ is used to define points on the curve, and $t$ denotes time. A rod can be viewed as a set of points on a curve plus two local director vectors. At any instant, the two director vectors are defined as

$$
\mathbf{a}_{\alpha}(\theta,t)
$$
